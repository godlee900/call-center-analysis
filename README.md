# Call Center Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Call Center Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence solution designed to monitor, analyze, and improve customer support operations. The dashboard provides insights into call volumes, customer satisfaction (CSAT), sentiment trends, geographic distribution, communication channels, and call reasons.

Built using a data visualization platform such as **Power BI**, this project helps managers and stakeholders make data-driven decisions to enhance customer experience and operational efficiency.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Monitor overall call center performance.
* Analyze customer sentiment and satisfaction levels.
* Identify major reasons customers contact support.
* Track call volume trends across days, cities, and states.
* Evaluate channel performance (Call Center, Chatbot, Email, Web).
* Support strategic decision-making through visual analytics.

## 📈 Key Insights

1. Over **32,000 customer interactions** were analyzed.
2. Average call duration is approximately **25 minutes**.
3. Negative sentiment dominates customer interactions.
4. Billing questions are the primary reason for contacting support.
5. California and Texas generate the highest call volumes.
6. Call Center remains the most utilized support channel.
7. Higher customer satisfaction strongly correlates with positive sentiment.
